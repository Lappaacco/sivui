1. Information on the service provider, service unit, and operations

1.1 Provider details
The service provider is Satu Paunonen, operating as a private healthcare professional. The service name is Ilojaloin Podiatry Services, business ID 3288544-8, registered at Pitkänlahdenkatu 13, 65100 Vaasa.

1.2 Service unit details
The service unit operates as a single professional clinic in Vaasa. No other employees or responsible persons are involved in the operations; Satu Paunonen is responsible for the complete operation.

1.3 Services, mission, and operating principles
Services include podiatric examination and treatment, assessment of skin and nail conditions, orthotic and toe support preparation, guidance, and preventive foot care. Services are provided by appointment at the clinic and do not include home visits or remote care.

1.4 Date
This public description was updated on August 4, 2026. The provider holds valid patient insurance and professional liability insurance.
