Public Description of Self-Monitoring

Ilojaloin Podiatry Services — Satu Paunonen

We provide individualized and safe podiatry services. This page contains the formal public description of the self-monitoring plan and its key operational principles. The full self-monitoring plan is available for download as a PDF.

This page is intended for client information and for review by authorities.

1. Services, mission, and operating principles

Ilojaloin Podiatry Services provides private podiatry healthcare services for clients of all ages. The service is used particularly by older adults, working-age adults, children, and young people, as well as clients with long-term conditions that affect foot health, such as diabetes, rheumatic diseases, neurological disorders, circulatory conditions, or skin and nail changes.

The services include podiatric examination and assessment, individualized podiatry care, treatment of skin and nail disorders, nail correction treatments, nail prostheses, preparation of individualized orthotic insoles, toe orthoses, client guidance and counseling, and prevention and self-care guidance related to foot health. The service does not include wound care and does not involve home visits or remote consultations.

Services are delivered by appointment at the service unit's premises in Vaasa. Each client's care is based on individualized assessment, clinical examination, and a treatment plan agreed with the client.

The aim of the service is to maintain and improve clients' foot health, functional capacity, and well-being using evidence-based methods. The care process emphasizes client-centeredness, safety, professionalism, confidentiality, and client participation in decisions concerning their own treatment.

3. Practical implementation of self-monitoring

Self-monitoring is based on continuous operational monitoring and proactive risk management. Activities are assessed regularly on the basis of client feedback and internal observations. In daily work, particular attention is given to safe treatment of clients, appropriate hygiene practices, maintenance and sterilization of instruments, accurate documentation in patient records, implementation of data protection, safety of the premises, safe use of medical devices, and the functionality of appointment booking and continuity of care.

Any deviations, near-miss incidents, and events that compromise client safety are recorded, analyzed, and evaluated to determine whether operational practices need to be changed.

4. Privacy, information security, and record management

  • Patient and personal data are processed confidentially and in accordance with applicable legislation.
  • Patient records are entered into the electronic Vello patient information system, which is the primary processing system.
  • Patient data is stored in the national Kanta service in accordance with applicable regulations.
  • Access to patient information is restricted to the service provider, since the service unit employs only one licensed healthcare professional.

5. Client and patient safety and risk management

Ilojaloin Podiatry Services is founded on safe, high-quality, evidence-based care. Patient safety is considered at every stage of treatment, from appointment booking to implementation, documentation, and follow-up guidance.

Before treatment begins, the client's health status, underlying conditions, medication, allergies, and other factors affecting care are assessed. Treatment is planned individually based on the client's health status, functional capacity, and goals.

Operational risks are assessed continuously. Particular attention is paid to client and patient safety, hygiene and infection prevention, reliability of equipment maintenance, functionality of medical devices, information security and privacy, safety of lone working, safety of the premises, and reliability of electronic systems.

6. Service availability, quality, and continuity

Ilojaloin Podiatry Services offers appointment-based podiatry services at its clinic in Vaasa. Services are available to clients of all ages, and both new and previously treated clients are accepted.

Bookings are primarily made electronically through the Vello patient information system. Clients may also book appointments by phone or message. The booking calendar is generally open 2–3 months ahead, allowing for planned treatment and flexible scheduling.

Service quality is monitored continuously as part of daily operations. Quality assurance is based on maintaining professional competence, using evidence-based treatment methods, careful documentation, and safe working practices. Service quality is assessed using client feedback, internal observations, handling of incidents and near misses, annual self-monitoring review, participation in continuing education, monitoring of equipment maintenance and hygiene practices, and assessment of privacy and information security compliance.

7. Maintenance of the self-monitoring plan

The self-monitoring plan is reviewed at least once a year and whenever the range of services changes, new working methods or equipment are introduced, legislation or official guidance changes, a safety or quality-related deviation is identified, or the risk assessment reveals new development needs.

Updates are versioned and previous versions are kept electronically. The self-monitoring plan is intended to be publicly available.
